About the Project

What is DIANA?

DIANA stands for Digital twin and Artificial Intelligence for adaptive maNAgement of pediatrics with type 1 diabetes. The project addresses a critical gap: existing decision-support tools for Type 1 Diabetes management are not designed for the unique physiological and lifestyle variability of children and adolescents.

🧬

Personalized Digital Twins

Patient-specific digital twin models built with ReplayBG to simulate individual glucose-insulin dynamics, continuously updated from wearable sensor data.

🤖

AI Decision Support

Multi-component decision support system covering basal insulin dosing, meal bolus management, physical activity recommendations, and safety interventions.

📱

Wearable Integration

Seamless connection to continuous glucose monitors, activity trackers, and smart insulin pens, feeding real-time data into the platform.

⚖️

Regulatory Compliance

Designed from the ground up for AI-Act, GDPR, and Medical Device Regulation compliance, ensuring safety and explainability throughout.

🏥

Clinical Validation

Rigorous 6-month randomized clinical trial with 150 pediatric participants to validate efficacy and safety of the platform in real-world settings.

🌐

Open-Source Release

Upon project completion, the full platform will be released as an open-source solution to maximize impact and enable adoption by healthcare systems worldwide.

Why Pediatric T1D Needs DIANA

Children and adolescents with Type 1 Diabetes face challenges that adult-focused tools cannot address: rapid growth, puberty-induced insulin resistance, highly variable physical activity, unpredictable meal patterns, and the involvement of caregivers in clinical decisions. DIANA is designed from the ground up around these realities, co-designed with patients, families, and clinicians to ensure it is both clinically effective and practically usable.

Timeline

Project Milestones

DIANA follows a structured 48-month roadmap from co-design with stakeholders through clinical validation and open-source release.

Project Launch

🚀 Launching Soon
May 30, 2026

Official start of the DIANA project. Research team onboarding, initial co-design preparation, and infrastructure setup.

M1 — Co-design Completion

Month 3
August 2026

Completion of participatory co-design sessions with patients, caregivers, and clinical partners to define platform requirements and workflows.

M2 — Digital Twin Framework Refined

Month 9
February 2027

Pediatric-specific digital twin models validated using retrospective datasets. ReplayBG framework adapted for growth and developmental variability.

M3 — DSS Modules Integrated

Month 18
November 2027

All decision support system modules (basal, bolus, activity, safety) integrated into the mobile and web platform with full wearable connectivity.

M4 — Full Platform Validated

Month 24
May 2028

Pre-clinical validation complete. Platform ready for regulatory review and clinical trial preparation.

M5 — Clinical Trial Protocol Approved

Month 27
August 2028

Ethics committee and regulatory approval obtained for the 6-month randomized controlled trial with 150 pediatric participants.

M6 — Clinical Trial Completed

Month 45
February 2030

6-month RCT with 150 children and adolescents with T1D completed. Primary endpoints evaluated and results submitted for publication.

M7 — Open-Source Release

Month 48
May 2030

Full DIANA platform released as open-source software. Documentation, datasets, and trained models made publicly available to the research community.
